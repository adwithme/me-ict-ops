## How To Remove Permission From Shared File And Folder For All Shared Person?

1. Move your cursor to the file/folder to be shared
2. Click on **Three dots** (•••) and click **Manage Access**
3. Click **Stop Sharing**
4. Confirm by clicking **Stop Sharing** again

## How To Remove Permission From Shared File And Folder? (Individual User)

1. Move your cursor to the file/folder
2. Click on **Three dots** (•••) and click **Manage Access**
3. Go to **Links Giving Access** section
4. Click on the **user icon** (just below "People you can specify can edit")
4. Click **Remove** button (X)
5. Confirm **Remove**

## How To Delete Files And Folder On OneDrive Web?

1. Select files/folders to delete
2. Click **Delete** (top of page) OR click **Three dots** → **Delete**
3. Confirm **Delete**

> **Note**: Deleted items move to **Recycle Bin**, retained for **90 days** only.

## How To See Shared Files And Folders With You?

1. Click **Shared** (left navigation)
2. Click **Shared with you**

## How To See Files And Folders Shared By You?

1. Click **Shared** (left navigation)
2. Click **Shared by you**

## What To Do When Your Files Are Replaced With Another Edited File Or Different Content With Same Name?

1. Move cursor to the replaced file
2. Click **Three dots** → **Version History**
3. Choose a version by **Modified Date**
4. Click **Three dots** on that version
5. Click **Restore**

## How To Check Your OneDrive Storage?

1. Click **Settings** icon (top right, gear)
2. Click **OneDrive Settings**
3. Click **More Settings**
4. Click **Storage Metrics**
5. Storage details shown on top right
