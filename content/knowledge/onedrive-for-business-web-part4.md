## Setting Up OneDrive On Your Windows Machine

### How To Setup OneDrive On Windows OS?

1. Click **Up Arrow** in Notification Area (system tray)
2. Click **OneDrive** icon (cloud)
3. Click **Sign In**
4. Enter your email address (e.g., `user@domain.com`) → **Enter**
5. Enter password → **Enter**
6. Choose OneDrive folder location → **Change Location** → Select drive (e.g., `D:\`) → **Next**
7. Click **Next** through remaining screens
8. Click **Finish**

> Your OneDrive is now ready.

---

### How To Configure Your OneDrive?

1. Click **Up Arrow** in Notification Area → **OneDrive** icon
2. Click **Help & Settings** → **Settings**
3. **Settings tab**:
   - ✅ **Save space and download files as you use them** (Files On-Demand)
   - ❌ **Start OneDrive automatically when I sign in to Windows** (optional)
4. Click **OK**

---

### How To Manage Backup Of Desktop, Documents, And Pictures?

1. **OneDrive** icon → **Help & Settings** → **Settings**
2. **Backup** tab → **Manage Backup**
3. Select folders: **Desktop**, **Documents**, **Pictures**
4. Click **Start Backup**
5. Close dialog → **OK**

---

### How To Add Second OneDrive (Personal + Business)?

1. **OneDrive** icon → **Help & Settings** → **Settings**
2. **Account** tab → **Add an account**
3. Follow setup steps above

---

### How To Share Files Using OneDrive App?

1. Open File Explorer → Navigate to file
2. **Right-click** file → **Share**
3. Enter email address and message
4. Click **Send**

---

### What Is The Use Of "Always Keep On Device" And "Free Up Space"?

#### Always Keep On Device
- Makes files available **offline** on your PC
- Shows **solid green circle with white check mark**
- Uses local disk space

#### Free Up Space
- Removes local copy, keeps file **online-only** (cloud)
- Does **not** delete from OneDrive
- Shows **blue cloud icon**

---

### What Do The OneDrive Icons Mean?

| Icon | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Red circle with white cross** | Sync error - file/folder cannot sync |
| **People icon** | File/folder shared with others |
| **Blue cloud** | Online-only (doesn't use local space) |
| **Green tick** | Locally available (downloaded) |
| **Solid green circle with white check** | "Always keep on this device" |
| **Padlock** | File has settings preventing sync (sensitivity label, etc.) |
