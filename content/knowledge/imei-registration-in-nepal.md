Nepal's **Mobile Device Management System (MDMS)** requires all mobile devices to be registered for legal use on Nepali networks. This guide walks you through the online registration process.

## Prerequisites

- **IMEI number(s)** - Dial `*#06#` on your phone
- **Passport/Citizenship** - For verification
- **Email & Phone** - For OTP and notifications
- **Payment method** - For applicable fees

## Step-by-Step Registration

### 1. Visit MDMS Portal

Go to: **`https://mdms.nta.gov.np`**

### 2. Click "Register Device"

### 3. Enter Device Details

- **IMEI 1** (primary)
- **IMEI 2** (if dual SIM)
- **Brand** (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, etc.)
- **Model** (iPhone 13, Galaxy S22, etc.)

### 4. Enter Owner Details

- **Full Name** (as per citizenship/passport)
- **Citizenship Number** or **Passport Number**
- **Email Address**
- **Mobile Number** (for OTP)

### 5. Verify OTP

Enter OTP sent to your email/mobile.

### 6. Pay Fee (If Applicable)

| Category | Fee |
|----------|-----|
| **Personal use (1 device)** | Free |
| **Additional devices** | NPR 200/device |
| **Commercial/Import** | Varies |

Payment via: eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay, or bank.

### 7. Submit & Save Acknowledgement

Download/print the registration certificate.

## Check Registration Status

Visit: `https://mdms.nta.gov.np/status` → Enter IMEI

## Important Notes

- **Deadline**: Register within 30 days of bringing device to Nepal
- **Unregistered devices**: Blocked from Nepali networks (Ncell, Ntc, Smart)
- **Tourists**: Temporary registration available (90 days)
- **Second-hand devices**: Re-register in new owner's name

## Common Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| "IMEI already registered" | Contact NTA with proof of ownership |
| OTP not received | Check spam, retry after 2 min |
| Payment failed | Try different gateway, check balance |
| Model not found | Select "Other" and enter manually |
